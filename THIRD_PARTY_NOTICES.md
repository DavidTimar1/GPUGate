# Third-party notices

GPUGate includes third-party code and fonts in three places:

- **The browser page** under `ui/vendor/`: htmx and the IBM Plex font files, served as they are.
- **Go modules** compiled into the `gpugate` binary when you build it. Their source is not in this repository: `go.mod` and `go.sum` name them and the Go tool downloads them.
- **The Go standard library and runtime**, also compiled into the binary.

Each licence text below is copied unchanged from the component's own published file.
